Above white: does a screenshot keep what the screen shows beyond ordinary white?

A screenshot file holds the screen's ordinary range. The screen can show more, and the eye sees it. Here that extra range is used on purpose. Take one screenshot of this whole page with the card playing, then one with the photo showing, and send both back.

1. The test card (public, no PIN)

Thirteen bands, each at the brightness it names, from 50 to 2000 nits. The labels are at ordinary white. If the phone clips, every band above ordinary white is flat white in the screenshot and the labels on them vanish into it. If the phone squeezes, the bands stay distinguishable.

2. The photo above white (PIN)

Every pixel of the photo sits between 250 and 1000 nits, above ordinary white. On an HDR screen it looks like a bright, slightly washed photo. In a clipping screenshot it is a flat white rectangle.
