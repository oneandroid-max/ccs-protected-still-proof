Apple check: does Safari keep protected video out of a screenshot?

For an iPhone, iPad or Mac, in Safari. Three short videos play side by side below. The first two go through Apple's protected path (FairPlay): Axinom's own test film, and our test card, packaged by the same code that packages the site's pictures. The third is plain, as a control.

  1. Wait until all three are moving (about ten seconds).
  2. Take a screenshot (iPhone: side button + volume up; Mac: Shift-Command-3).
  3. Open the screenshot in Photos. Both protected ones should be black; the control should show the film. If the control is black too, the screenshot proves nothing on that device.
  4. If you can, also try a screen recording of all three.
