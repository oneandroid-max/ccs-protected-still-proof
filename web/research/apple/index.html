Apple check: does Safari keep protected video out of a screenshot?

For an iPhone, iPad or Mac, in Safari. Four short videos play below. Three go through Apple's protected path (FairPlay): Axinom's own test film; our test card, packaged by the same code that packages the site's pictures, under Axinom's public test key; and the same card under a key of its own with a licence from OUR Axinom account, exactly as a picture would get one (shown once that account exists). The last is plain, as a control.

  1. Wait until they are moving (about ten seconds).
  2. Take a screenshot (iPhone: side button + volume up; Mac: Shift-Command-3).
  3. Open the screenshot in Photos. The protected ones should be black; the control should show the film. A black one counts only if the line under it says playing with a size (for example 720x960) and its time moving; if it says no frame, nothing was playing there, and if the control is black too, the screenshot proves nothing on that device.
  4. If you can, also try a screen recording of all of them.
