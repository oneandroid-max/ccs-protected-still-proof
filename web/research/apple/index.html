Apple check: does Safari keep protected video out of a screenshot?

For an iPhone, iPad or Mac, in Safari. Two copies of the same short film play below: the first through Apple's protected path (FairPlay), the second plain, as a control.

  1. Wait until both are moving (about ten seconds).
  2. Take a screenshot (iPhone: side button + volume up; Mac: Shift-Command-3).
  3. Open the screenshot in Photos. The protected one should be black; the control should show the film. If both are black, the screenshot proves nothing on that device.
  4. If you can, also try a screen recording of both.
