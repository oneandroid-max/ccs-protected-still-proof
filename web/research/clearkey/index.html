Candidate 4: the still as a ClearKey-encrypted video

The photo is a two-second video encrypted with the standard ClearKey system. No licence server: this page derives the key from the link key and the PIN and hands it to the browser itself. The first proof's Widevine video came out black on Android because Widevine decrypts inside the phone's protected path; ClearKey decrypts in the browser's own software, so the expectation is that the screenshot shows the frame. The screenshot you take is the measurement.
