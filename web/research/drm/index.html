Route 1: the photo through the browser's protected video path

No install. The photo is a two-second video encrypted with Common Encryption, and the browser's own DRM (Widevine in Chrome, PlayReady in Edge) decrypts it inside the device's protected path. Where the device grants hardware protection, the phone or PC withholds the frame from every capture; where it grants only software decryption, the capture shows the photo. This page prints what the device grants and the capture you take is the measurement. The keys here are the vendors' public test keys, so they protect nothing; the real build binds every licence to the viewer's session on CCS's own licence service.
