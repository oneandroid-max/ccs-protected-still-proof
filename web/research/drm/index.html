Route 1: the browser's own protected video path

No install. A short video is encrypted with Common Encryption and the browser's own DRM (Widevine in Chrome, PlayReady in Edge) decrypts it inside the device's protected path. Where the device grants hardware protection the phone or PC withholds the frame from every capture; where it grants only software decryption the capture shows the video. What plays here is a labeled test pattern, not the portrait, because this page is measuring one thing: whether your device's protected path blacks out a capture. The keys are the vendors' public test keys and protect nothing. Read the result first, then tap Play.
