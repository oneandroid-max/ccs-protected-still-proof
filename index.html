Try it

  1. Wait until "Playing" reads yes and the photo is on screen.
  2. Take a screenshot with the phone's buttons, then open it from your gallery. The photo area should be black; the rest of the page is captured as normal.
  3. Start a screen recording, then stop it and play it back. The photo area should be black.
