What this page cannot do

This page cannot make a capture come out black, and no web page can: the browser hands every frame to the device, and nothing inside a page can refuse the device's own capture. That is why the photo is not here. The viewer's refusal is the device's own, asked for by our program; a camera pointed at any screen still photographs it, and that photo carries the viewer's mark. The ccsproof:// link can be claimed by any installed app; the PIN still gates the photo.
